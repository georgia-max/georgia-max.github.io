## LLMs & evaluation

**Measuring Similarity in Causal Graphs: A Framework for Semantic and Structural Analysis**<br>
Liu, N., Yang, F., & Jalali, M. S. (2025). *arXiv:2503.11046*.<br>
Reviews 40+ similarity metrics, selects 9, and tests them on 2,000 LLM-generated causal graphs.<br>
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11046) · [Code](https://github.com/georgia-max/Causalgraph-comparison)

**Leveraging Large Language Models for Automated Causal Loop Diagram Generation: Enhancing System Dynamics Modeling through Curated Prompting Techniques**<br>
Liu, N., & Keith, D. (2025). *arXiv:2503.21798*.<br>
A curated prompting approach for turning qualitative text into causal loop diagrams.<br>
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21798)

## Human–automation interaction

**The Relationships among Workload, Automation Reliance, and Human Errors in Safety-Critical Monitoring Roles**<br>
Liu, N., Triantis, K., Madsen, P., & Roets, B. (2025). *Safety Science*, 185, 106775.<br>
Uses operational data from Belgian railway traffic control to show how workload and reliance on automation drive human error.<br>
[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssci.2024.106775)

**A Multi-dimensional Index of Evaluating Systems Thinking Skills from Textual Data**<br>
Liu, N., Mahmoudi, H., Triantis, K., & Ghaffarzadegan, N. (2024). *Systems Research and Behavioral Science*.<br>
Scores systems-thinking skill from open-ended written responses.<br>
[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/sres.3033) · [PDF](https://georgia-max.github.io/files/paper_multindex.pdf)

**Workload Dynamics in Safety-Critical Monitoring Roles: Evidence from the Belgian Railway Network**<br>
Liu, N., Triantis, K., & Roets, B. (2024). *SSRN working paper*.<br>
[Paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4798034)
