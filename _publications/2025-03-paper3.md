I developed and tested prompting strategies for translating dynamic hypotheses (text) into causal loop diagrams (CLDs) using large language models (LLMs). With curated prompting, LLMs can generate CLDs comparable in quality to expert-built ones, cutting expert analysis time 10x and lowering the barrier for novice system dynamicists.
